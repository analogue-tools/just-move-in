## 2. Voice & channel stance (explicit)

Survey preference for voice as a primary interaction was **0** (n=12). Brief / ops reality still needs access capture on Day 0.

**Decision in this case:** provide **both**, UI-first Day 0 capture always; **voice as an optional path** for access / hands-busy moments. Never voice-only. Never chatbot front door.
