## A · Getting started

### Who are you and why do I have this?
Your letting agent or partner referred your move to Just Move In. We help set up the admin around a new home: council, water, energy, broadband and the rest. Nothing is sent to suppliers until contracts have exchanged (or your tenancy start is confirmed) and you have agreed what we should do.

### Do I have to use this?
No. You can ignore messages, opt out in one tap, or talk to your named specialist. Opt-out applies across channels.

### Is Jay a real person?
Jay is the digital move operator. Your named Move Specialist (for example customer support human agent) is a real person who owns escalations. We say which is which on every hard screen.

### How do you get paid?
Suppliers on our panel pay Just Move In a panel fee. **It does not change your price.** We use the same rule across the panel. Free local listings are labelled as earning us nothing.

## B · Before exchange (The Fog)

### Why is there nothing to do yet?
Until exchange (or a confirmed tenancy start), the date can still fall through. Acting early means repeating work and contacting organisations about a move that may not happen. We watch for the date and show you the map of what is coming.

### Will you call me before I am ready?
Contact is capped while your move is only being watched. You can opt out any time. We store where consent came from and when.

## C · Choices (energy, broadband, insurance)

### Why am I only seeing one recommendation?
A single clear pick is easier to act on than a long comparison list. You can still swipe to see alternatives. We explain why this pick fits your tenancy length, household and timing.

### Is this the whole market?
No. It is our panel at your address. We show how many providers are on the panel and how many are available for your property.

### Can I choose something else?
Yes. Open alternatives, decline, or ask your specialist. We will not re-present a recommendation you have already declined.

### Will you switch me without asking?
No. Anything involving money or a contract needs your confirmation. Routine notifications (council, water, TV licence, redirect) can run under a Letter of Authority you sign first.

## D · After you confirm (receipts and status)

### How do I know something actually happened?
Every task has a real state: queued (with send date), sent, chasing, confirmed, or sent with no receipt when the organisation does not confirm. A green confirmed state requires a stored receipt.

### Why does it say "sent · no receipt"?
Some councils and water companies do not send a confirmation we can store. We still show that we sent it, when we will chase, and we avoid pretending it is confirmed.

### Why haven't you notified anyone yet?
Notifications often go out on a schedule after your move date (your plan shows the send date on each queued task). If something looks wrong, open the task or call your specialist.

### What if you acted on the wrong address or person?
Tell us immediately via Connect to customer support human agent. We resolve liability (property, liable person, start date, agreement type) before tasks fire; if something still goes wrong, that is an escalation, not a FAQ.

## E · Moving day

### Why a phone call on the day?
You often have no broadband and one free hand. Voice leads for two things only: confirming you have the keys, and capturing meter readings. The same actions stay available as buttons in the app.

### Will Jay take payment on the call?
No. No card details, no direct debit, no one-time passcodes by voice. Payment always happens on a link you open yourself.

### What if I miss the call?
Use the on-screen buttons, reschedule, or call your specialist. Meter readings should still be captured with a photo and a read-back before anything is submitted.

## F · Date changes

### My completion date moved. Do I start again?
No. Change the date once. The plan recalculates. You see what was rescheduled, what needs redoing, and nothing should be silently lost. If a supplier cannot amend (for example a broadband slot inside 48 hours), we name exactly what you need to do.

## G · Local services and settling

### What is the Market tab for?
Practical help near your new address: cleaning, handyman, packing and similar. Search and map stay in sync. Listings show ratings and guarantees up front.

### Do you earn money if I book?
Sometimes. When we do, we use the same panel-fee / earnings note. Free help is labelled clearly.

### When will this feel like home?
There is no deadline and no streak. Your Home board fills in as things complete and never decays if you are slow. The first weeks are for settling, not perfection.

---

## H · Privacy, safety, vulnerability

### How do I stop contact?
One-tap opt-out in the app or message your specialist. It should apply to every channel quickly.

### I need extra support (Priority Services, medical equipment, accessibility)
Say so in discovery or to your specialist. We check meter type and vulnerability before energy switches and can offer Priority Services Register routes. If the product is hard to use, ask for a human-led path.

### Something feels like a scam
Hang up on unexpected payment requests. Check the named specialist and the in-app receipt. We will never ask for bank details on an unsolicited call.
