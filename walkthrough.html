Feature 01

Event triggers, not dates

Activation waits for exchange. A date change fires a cascade that redraws the plan with nothing re-entered.

Feature 02

One brief, many baskets

Overlapping questions asked once, fanned out across energy, broadband and insurance.

Feature 03

One pick, not a grid

A single recommendation per category with a reason. Alternatives collapse behind an expander.

Feature 04

Honest task states

Confirmed, or sent with no receipt. Never a green tick we cannot substantiate.

Feature 05

Commission on the card

What we earn sits in the same block as the social proof, at the point of choice.

Feature 06

Voice on moving day only

Two intents, scoped tight: keys confirmation and meter capture. No broadband needed.

Feature 07

Two lists, two registers

Must do is deadpan. Could do is warm and untimed. Same data, different tone.

Feature 08

A named human throughout

One specialist owns the move and receives every escalation. Reachable in one tap.
