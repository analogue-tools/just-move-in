Design exploration · step 1 of 2

Four type and colour directions

Each one is rendered on the same three components, a task row, an offer card and a consequence stat, so they can be compared like for like rather than as abstract swatches. None of these are Just Move In branded yet. That is the next step.

Research 01

This product is half government admin

Council tax, DVLA, HMRC, electoral roll. GOV.UK uses GDS Transport, adapted from the Kinneir and Calvert road sign face designed to be read at speed in poor conditions. That lineage is the right reference point, not a fintech one.

Research 02

14px and below is an accessibility problem

GOV.UK removed 14 from its type scale entirely, citing British Dyslexia Association guidance of no smaller than 12pt, which is 16px. Line heights sit in multiples of 5px for vertical rhythm. Source.

Research 03

Money needs tabular lining figures

font-variant-numeric: lining-nums tabular-nums is the standard for financial interfaces. Every digit takes the same width, so columns align and figures do not jump when they update. Proportional stays in body copy.

01

Civic

Direction 01

Civic

Reads as a public service that happens to be well made. Highest legibility of the four, lowest personality. Atkinson Hyperlegible was designed by the Braille Institute specifically to disambiguate characters people confuse at low vision: capital I, lowercase l, the numeral 1, and O against zero.

Display Atkinson Hyperlegible Bold UI Atkinson Hyperlegible Data IBM Plex Mono Accent Ink blue, not violet

Strongest for: the disabled and novice movers who show up unprompted in the reviews as the people who need this most. Weakest for: feeling like a modern consumer product. It is a little municipal.

Editorial

Direction 03

Editorial

The differentiated one. A serif for display and a neo-grotesque for interface, on warm paper rather than cool grey. Reads as considered advice rather than an app, which suits a product whose whole argument is honesty about what has actually happened.

Direction 04

Systems

Precise and slightly technical. Space Grotesk has distinctive numerals and a mechanical edge that suits a product built around visible machine state. This is the direction that leans hardest into "it is a robot and it gets things done".

05

Why the numbers need fixing whichever direction wins

Proportional figures, the default

The 1 is narrower than the 9, so the right edge wanders and the eye cannot compare down the column. Watch what happens when a figure updates.

  • Energy£118.00
  • Broadband£25.00
  • Contents cover£9.00
  • Per month£152.00

Tabular lining figures

Every digit takes the same width, so columns align and nothing shifts when a value changes. Trailing zeros kept so the string length is stable. One CSS line.

The type scale, corrected

Body · 16/25Council tax runs daily from the day you move in. Camden adds £70 if it is not registered within 21 days.
Small · 16/20We earn £38 if you switch. It does not change your price.
Metadata · 14/20Jay sent it · waiting on CamdenFloor. Never below this, and never for anything the mover must read to act.

This is a correction to my own prototype. I used 10, 11 and 12.5px for states, labels and task reasons. GOV.UK removed 14px from its scale entirely on accessibility grounds, and the segment that surfaced unprompted in your reviews as needing this service most is disabled movers. Small type is the wrong place to be clever here, so the floor goes to 14px for metadata and 16px for anything read to make a decision.

What we fold in next

Read from the current Figma screens. Step two is applying these to whichever direction you pick, rather than replacing them.

Primary violet, buttons and Jay Cool lilac ground White cards, ~20px radius Savings green Pill tint, Top Pick

Also carried over: generous corner radii, the chip and pill vocabulary for attributes, the sparkle on the Jay wordmark, the bottom tab bar, and the flat human illustration style on the promotional cards. The one thing I would not carry over is the very light grey display type in the current mock, because it fails contrast badly.

If you want my pick

Direction 03, Editorial, with Just Move In's violet swapped in for the indigo. Everything else in this project argues that the differentiator is honesty rather than friendliness, and the warm paper plus serif display carries that better than another rounded purple app. It also puts real distance between us and Homebox, who occupy Direction 02 already.

01If the brand team says a serif is too far, Direction 02 with the Civic type scale is the safe compromise. Keep the existing look, fix the sizes.

02Borrow one thing from Civic regardless of the winner: Atkinson Hyperlegible as the accessible alternate font, offered as a setting. Cheap to add, and it is the segment your own reviews say needs this most.

03Direction 04 is the one I would not pick despite liking it. It reads as competent machinery, and the person using this at 11pm before completion is anxious, not impressed by machinery.
