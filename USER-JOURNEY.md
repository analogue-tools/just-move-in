# The move, step by step
### What happens today, what happens with Jay, and where each bit actually happens
**Jeanne Piffaut · July 2026**

---

**The thing this table shows.** There are 38 steps in a UK move. Today the mover does 34 of them. With Jay they do 11, and only 7 of those happen in an app.

Most of this product has no interface. That's the point. The screens exist for the two things a person has to do themselves: understand a choice, and approve one. Everything else should be invisible or arrive as a text message.

**Channel key:** `invisible` nothing to see, it just happens · `app` a screen · `sms` a text · `voice` a phone call · `world` physically happening in real life · `3rd party` the mover still deals with someone else

## Stage 1 · The Fog · offer accepted to exchange

The date isn't real yet. 37% of sales at this stage never complete, so the right move is almost nothing.

## Three things this table makes obvious

**Most of the product isn't a screen.** 12 steps go from manual to invisible. That's the actual value, and it's the part that photographs badly in a demo. Worth saying out loud when presenting, or the room judges it on the 7 screens alone.

**Voice earns its place at exactly three steps.** 24, 27 and 28. All on moving day, all with no broadband and one free hand. That's the whole case for voice, and it's also the moment Just Move In currently doesn't touch at all.

**Step 23 is the one nobody else has.** Every competitor's version of this table has a hole where the date change goes. Comparison sites don't know your date. Address change services don't know your date. The US category leader is a dashboard that waits to be opened. The date moving is the most ordinary thing that happens in a UK move, and it's the thing the entire market ignores.
